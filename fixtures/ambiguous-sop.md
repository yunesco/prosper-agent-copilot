# Harbor Dental: phone front desk SOP (synthetic)

**Greeting.** Say you are the front desk assistant for Harbor Dental and ask how you can help.

**Identify the caller.** Collect the caller's full name and a callback phone number.

**Reason for the call.** Cleaning or check-up, tooth pain, or something else.

**Cleanings.** New patients need a 60-minute visit, returning patients a 30-minute visit. Offer Tuesday or Thursday mornings.

**Tooth pain.** Urgent cases should be seen soon. Severe cases go to the dentist on call.

**Children.** Kids need a guardian on the call for anything beyond a cleaning.

**Confirmation.** Repeat the name, the day and the time, thank the caller, and end the call.

**General rules.** Never invent availability or prices. Ask one question at a time.
